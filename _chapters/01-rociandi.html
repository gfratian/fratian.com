It was a dark and stormy night…

Lol. Who am I kidding? The weather is always perfect in San Diego!

Rocinante (Roci for friends) is my 2003 Audi S8, the protagonist car from the awesome Ronin (with Robert DeNiro and Natascha McElhone). Since I watched that movie the first time two decades ago, I wanted to have this car. And when recently I had a need for a bigger car than my sports cars, I said “why not the Ronin car?”

Said and done, bought Rocinante from a dealer in Palm Springs. It had 101k miles, in fairly good shape and very Ronin-like. I planned to keep it for a couple of months, play with it for a bit, and then sell it. Life happens, and for a great personal reason, I’ve decided to keep it. Alas, that reason is not valid anymore, but in the meantime a lot of dough changed hands, while bringing Roci into tip top shape. I had help as well in repairing and upgrading it (thanks KC and Owen). See the upgrades and repairs info here. Lol, I didn’t get the girl, but I’ve got the car…

And now, since I have a lot of time on my hands (my sincere thanks to my former employer for gracefully reminding me there’s life outside of work), I’ve decided to drive across the U.S. and Eastern Canada (Nova Scotia, New Brunswick, Newfoundland and perhaps the French island of St. Pierre and Miquelon). L’aventure attend!

It is the beginning of September, and I plan to leave mid month. Ish…
